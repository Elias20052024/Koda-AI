You are a senior Product Designer, UI/UX Designer, Full-Stack React Developer, and Software Architect.

Build a complete Minimum Viable Product (MVP) called **Koda AI**.

The goal is to create a modern, production-quality AI-powered internship matching platform for IT students, companies, and university partners.

The MVP should focus on validating the business idea with the minimum number of features while maintaining a premium SaaS experience.

=========================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
=========================================================

Koda AI helps IT students discover internship opportunities based on their technical skills instead of relying only on resumes.

Companies can post internships and discover suitable candidates using AI-powered matching.

Universities can verify students and monitor internship placements.

This is Phase 1.

Do NOT include:

• Freelancing
• Full-time jobs
• Part-time jobs
• Courses
• Certifications
• Marketplace
• Mentoring
• Chat
• Social networking
• Payments
